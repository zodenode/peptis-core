### Clear space

Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the lowercase “e” on all sides. Do not stretch, outline, recolor outside the palette, or place on busy photography without a dark/light scrim.

### Colorways

- **Primary:** Earthy sage green on transparent, paper, or forest
- Do not use the legacy burgundy wordmark for Peptis consumer surfaces
