# Approved high confidence statistics

Use the qualifier and source with each figure. Recheck the full dossier before expanding a claim.

- **Weight loss composition:** In the SURMOUNT 1 DXA substudy, about 75% of weight lost was fat
  mass and 25% was lean mass. Lean mass is not all skeletal muscle. Look et al., *Diabetes,
  Obesity and Metabolism*, 2025, dossier [12]. Substudy with 160 paired scans.
- **Broader GLP-1 estimate:** A network meta analysis of 22 randomized trials and 2,258
  participants estimated lean mass loss at about 25% of total weight lost. Drugs, populations and
  measurement methods varied. Karakasis et al., 2025, dossier [13].
- **Resistance training:** Resistance training attenuated lean mass loss during general weight
  loss by about 0.8 kg in an overview of 12 systematic reviews and 149 studies. This was not
  GLP-1 specific. Bellicha et al., *Obesity Reviews*, 2021, dossier [21].
- **Protein:** Reviews support roughly 1.2 to 1.6 g/kg/day and 25 to 30 g per meal for many active
  weight loss contexts. The weight denominator and medical status require individualization.
  Nunes et al., 2022 and Leidy et al., 2015, dossier [24, 25].
- **Maintenance:** One year after semaglutide and lifestyle support ended in the STEP 1 extension,
  participants regained about two thirds of prior weight loss. Frame this as chronic biology, not
  failure. Wilding et al., *Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism*, 2022, dossier [9].

Evidence reviewed through August 21, 2026. For education only. Individual results vary.
