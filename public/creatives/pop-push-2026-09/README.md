# Peptis Core pop and push creative pack

Eight distinct campaign concepts for the current Peptis Core Continuity funnel. The conversion
event is the free three-minute continuity check and optional `$0` founding reservation. The offer
is a waitlist and planning experience, not medical care, prescribing, medication, clinician review
or pharmacy fulfillment.

## Deliverables

Each numbered concept has:

- `pop/*-1200x628.jpg`: copy-complete prelander or landing-page hero creative.
- `push/images/*-720x480.jpg`: text-free push main image.
- `push/icons/*-192x192.png`: matching push icon.
- `source/*.jpg`: 1200 by 800 text-free source master for future crops.
- `campaigns.csv`: short push copy, pop copy, CTA, destination URL and test priority.
- `contact-sheet.jpg`: review sheet for all eight pop layouts.
- `PROMPTS.md`: the image-generation prompt set.

Pop traffic normally redirects to a destination rather than exposing a separate image field. Use
the `pop` assets as the visual above the fold on a prelander or campaign-specific landing variant.
Confirm the chosen network's current dimensions, character limits and health-ad policy before
uploading.

## Concepts

1. **Strength check:** everyday function after weight loss.
2. **Scale blind spot:** scale weight versus strength and routine.
3. **Routine and energy:** a calm reset without diagnosing a cause.
4. **Practical protein:** food-first, low-volume protein planning.
5. **Meal comfort:** organized notes without symptom dramatization.
6. **Next phase:** continuity and maintenance over time.
7. **Free check:** eight questions and a written summary.
8. **Founding reservation:** `$0` today, no payment details and a later choice.

## Suggested first test

Start with concepts 01, 02, 07 and 08 as four separate cells. They cover the clearest problem,
category thesis, low-friction tool and offer. Use concepts 03 through 06 as second-wave angle
tests. Do not mix creative and landing-page variants inside one cell.

## Guardrails

- Do not pair these assets with medication pens, before-and-after imagery, fake scarcity or
  guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not describe the reservation as care or imply that services are live.
- Keep `Not medical care today` or an equivalent capability caveat near the conversion point.
- Final paid acquisition requires network-policy review plus confirmation of Peptis domain,
  clinical, provider, pharmacy and state readiness.
- Pricing and availability may change before activation. Eligibility and affirmative enrollment
  are required if services launch.
